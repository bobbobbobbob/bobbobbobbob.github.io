Our Mission

Promoting Liberty Inside Ridgeway

Turning Point Ridgeway was founded to bring the principles of individual freedom, fiscal responsibility, and limited government to the streets and halls of Ridgeway.

We believe that every citizen of Ridgeway deserves a voice — and we're here to make sure that voice is loud, clear, and unapologetically pro-freedom.

Free Markets

Support local businesses and entrepreneurship in Ridgeway. No government overreach in the marketplace.

Individual Rights

Your freedoms are non-negotiable. We fight for every citizen's right to live without bureaucratic interference.

Constitutional Order

Ridgeway's laws must respect the founding principles. No compromise on the basics of ordered liberty.

Community First

Strong communities, not big government, are the bedrock of prosperity in Ridgeway.
